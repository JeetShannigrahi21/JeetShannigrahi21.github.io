      <h3>Master’s Thesis — Graduate Research Assistant</h3>
      <span class="date">Sep 2024 - Nov 2026</span>
    </div>
    <p class="meta"> The Quantum Matter Institute, The University of British Columbia, Canada · Supervisor: Prof. Mona Berciu</p>
    <ul>
      <li> Description: Studying the temperature dependence of the effective mass and lifetime of the Holstein and SSH polaron in cubic lattices across different dimensions through a quasi-analytic variational method called Momentum Average (MA).  At its lowest order, it treats the problem by truncating to the smallest physically significant variational space, which in real space, translates to limiting the phonon cloud to a single site. Higher orders involve sequentially enlarging the variational space by adding one or more remote phonons, systematically improving the accuracy of the method. </li>
      <li>Role: On an operational level, the MA method involves pen-and-paper calculations with Green’s functions and cleverly resuming Feynman diagrams to arrive at closed form expressions or a point from which numerical computations are relatively trivial. I analytically extended MA to finite temperatures, numerically validating the results obtained through Variational Exact Diagonalization (VED), DMRG, DMFT etc. Moreover, MA is relatively dimension-agnostic, allowing for a smooth generalization to higher dimensions (2D and 3D) where numerical data is lacking.</li>
      <li>Outcomes: Contributed talk at CAP Congress (June 2025); poster at the Max Planck&ndash;UBC&ndash;UTokyo Centre Summer School (May 2025) and at the First International Quantum Horizons Symposium (August 2025). </li>
    </ul>
